## 2. Commissioning vs. Stock Purchasing Guidelines

1. **When to Commission:**
   - Royal profile cover features (commission exclusive portrait sessions on medium-format digital, e.g. Hasselblad/Phase One).
   - Flagship hotel reviews (send an editorial photojournalist for quiet-luxury atmospheric imagery).
2. **When to License from Premium Archives (Bridgeman / Alamy Cultural / Getty Prestige):**
   - Historical dynastic timelines (18th and 19th-century royal miniature paintings, British Raj state visits, durbar ceremonies).
3. **When Open-Source Creative Commons Suffices:**
   - Wide palace exteriors, public ASI monument facades, UNESCO heritage ruins, and landscape geography where CC-BY-SA 4.0 captures 24MP+ architectural clarity.
